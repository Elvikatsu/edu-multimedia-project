Course Outcomes

Learning Goals

Upon completing all six curriculum modules on this portal, learners will be able to:

  • LG1

    Formulate Multimodal Representations

    Explain how raw pixels, video frames, audio waveforms, and text tokens are transformed into shared latent vector spaces.

  • LG2

    Analyze Vision & Acoustic Neural Architectures

    Compare convolutional, recurrent, and self-attention mechanisms (ViT, Whisper, CLIP) across visual and speech recognition benchmarks.

  • LG3

    Evaluate Generative Synthesis & Cross-Modal Alignment

    Understand adversarial training (GANs), iterative denoising diffusion (DDPM/LDM), and cross-attention captioning models.

  • LG4

    Apply Media Forensics & Provenance Standards

    Critically assess deepfake detection cues, cryptographic provenance (C2PA), and ethical copyright governance in generative media.
